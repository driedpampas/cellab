Finding the cycle, and what it tells us

Step 41 has the fingerprint of step 1.

The rules are deterministic, so from step 1 the run repeats every 40 steps forever.

One repeat answers stable, cycle, occurs, and any “state after N”.

This is the key fact from Deliverable 1: a finite grid has finitely many states, so a deterministic run must eventually repeat. Once it does, we know the entire future without simulating it.
