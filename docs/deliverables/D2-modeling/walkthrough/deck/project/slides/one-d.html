With fixed 0 edges on 63 cells, chaotic Rule 30 freezes completely from step 175. The questions found it: “Stable from step 175”.

The surprise came from the questions, not from looking at pictures: the experiment asked "stable?" and "cycle?", so the run kept going after its 31 steps until it could answer. With edges fixed at 0, the edges slowly freeze the pattern into vertical stripes, and at step 175 nothing changes any more. On a ring (wrap-around edges) Rule 30 does not freeze; it cycles, with the lengths from the Deliverable 1 slides. A good fact for the final presentation.
