The engine

How the engine computes one step

All cells update at once, from the old grid.

Neighbours are shifted copies of the grid: no loop over cells.

The first rule that applies to a cell decides its new value.

CelLab · Deliverable 2 · real values from the brief’s forest fire

The grid is padded once (wrap-around or a fixed edge value) and each neighbour becomes a shifted view of the padded grid, so every rule is evaluated for all cells together with NumPy. A 200 by 200 grid takes 1 to 3 milliseconds per step on our laptops.
