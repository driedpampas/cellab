CelLab · Deliverable 1

All 15 examples from the brief, grouped by what a cell needs to know. Neighbour rules are classic cellular automata and fit one simple loop. Numeric cells do arithmetic. Moving particles need an update order and a collision rule. Agents carry their own state (position, direction, energy) and act one at a time.
