CelLab · Deliverable 1 · hardest: agents and randomness

Patterns worth pointing out. Agents and randomness are the hardest to simulate and to ask questions about. Rule 30 is trivial to simulate but the hardest to learn, which is why it is a bonus item. Diffusion is just averaging, a linear operation, so a network can learn it exactly. Fire, epidemic and crystal growth always settle, because cells only ever change in one direction.
