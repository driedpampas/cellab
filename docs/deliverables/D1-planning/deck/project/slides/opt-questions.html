Options for answering the questions

OptionAdvantagesDisadvantages
Simulate up to a step limitTrivial to writeCannot prove "never"
Store a hash of every state (our choice)Exact for questions 2, 3 and 5Memory grows with cycle length
Floyd or Brent cycle detectionAlmost no memorySlower, more complex
Shortcuts from rule properties (where possible)Instant and exactOnly work for some rules
Random rules: fixed seed or many seedsExact replay, or statisticsStatistics are approximate

Reason for our choice: hashing is simple and exact. If a cycle is too long to keep in memory, we switch to Brent's method.

CelLab · Deliverable 1 · highlighted rows = our choice

Floyd's and Brent's algorithms find cycles while remembering only a couple of states, at the cost of extra steps; they are the fallback for very long cycles. For random automata, a fixed seed makes a run reproducible so all five questions work; running many seeds turns the answers into probabilities, which connects to the Bayesian networks lab.
