CelLab · Deliverable 1

This is our take on "answer the questions without running the automaton". Some answers can be proven from the rule itself: conserved quantities, rules that only go one way, and the known result (Goles and Olivos) that synchronous majority rules end in a fixed point or a 2-cycle. Everything else is either simulated exactly or predicted by the model, with its accuracy reported.
